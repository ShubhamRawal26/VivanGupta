Critical Risks

The 1% Mistake That Ruins Ranks

Scoring high in JEE is only half the battle. JoSAA and CSAB counseling is a rigid maze where a single wrong click can permanently cost you your dream college.

Wrong Choice Order

Placing a lower-tier college above a premium one permanently blocks you from better seats once allotted.

Document Invalidation

Outdated OBC-NCL or EWS certificates lead to instant category cancellation and forfeiture of allotted seats.

Premature Panic Settling

Students panic and freeze seats early in Round 2, completely missing out on massive rank drops during CSAB Special Rounds.

State Quota Misuse

Failing to align State-of-Eligibility with 12th board passing certificates, throwing away vital Home State rank privileges.
